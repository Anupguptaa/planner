Gardening Is Joyful – Until It Gets Messy

Most beginners don't struggle because they lack green thumbs. They struggle because everything lives in their head, on random sticky notes or in a pile of seed packets.

Common Beginner Challenges

  • ✕Not knowing what to plant, or when, so seeds go in too early or too late.
  • ✕Buying duplicate seeds because there's no inventory of what you already own.
  • ✕Forgetting watering and care routines, then wondering why plants struggle.
  • ✕Losing track of pests, what worked and what didn't from one season to the next.
  • ✕Overspending because there is no garden budget.
